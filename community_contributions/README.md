# Community contributions

Work shared by students of the course, one folder each, added by pull request.

At the end of Week 1 Day 3, share the documents you wrote that day. Copy your `intent.md`, `spec.md`, `plan.md` and `REVIEW.md` into a folder of your own here, add a short `README.md` that says what you built and links to your build in your fork, and open a pull request. Other students learn from seeing how you approached the same MVP.

Small, self-contained pieces are welcome too: a skill, a hook, a subagent, a CI workflow, or a guide for a coding agent the course doesn't cover. Keep screenshots and code in your fork and link to them, so the repo stays small.

The steps are in [course/for_agents/AGENT_GIT_GUIDE.md](../course/for_agents/AGENT_GIT_GUIDE.md), and your coding agent can take you through them.

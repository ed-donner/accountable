# Accountable MVP: splucena

My Week 1 Day 3 build of the Accountable MVP: a sales CRM for one person, with a dashboard, a drag-and-drop pipeline board, and searchable, sortable, paged tables and record pages for organizations, contacts and deals.

- Backend: FastAPI on SQLite through SQLAlchemy Core, with every stage-change rule in one shared service.
- Frontend: React and TypeScript, built from mockup B, with line, bar, pipeline and funnel charts on the dashboard.
- Tests: 161 pytest, 48 Vitest and 31 Playwright tests in Google Chrome.

The documents here are the day's intent, spec, plan and review policy. The full build is in my fork: https://github.com/splucena/accountable
